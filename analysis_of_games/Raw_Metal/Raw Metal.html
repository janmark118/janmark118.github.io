RAW METAL

Raw Metal is a blend of top down stealth action and beat'em up game genres. Here you play as an unnamed scavenger who infiltrated a mining megacorporation by the name of Orpheus Technologies on the exoplanet Charybdis . Here your best allies are the shadows, your wits and the many gadgets and gears you can find around the mining complex, though if things ever go south you will need to use your strength and agility to defeat your opponents. The game was developed by the indie studio Team Crucible , which is composed by: Corrigan Owen , Zackary Wilson , Pavel Kozulya, Jorden Harshaw , James Stokes . There are many things to love about this game: The funny and witty dialogue of the guards, the punishing yet rewarding fighting mechanics, the artstyle which mixes the steam punk aesthetic with a more futuristic look and the notable OST made by Zackary Willson which I from time to time listen. The only issue that I had with this game is that its trying to be a rogue like game but missing a core feature for this genre: procedurally generated area, which are missing in this game.
